# Datasets Knowledge

This skill provides a list of datasets used in the surveyed research papers. It can be used as a reference to know which datasets each paper evaluated on and what these datasets contain.

| Dataset Name | Number of Scenes | Contents / Description | 3D or 4D? | Camera Setup | Real / Synthetic | Data Format & Metadata | Primary Usecase |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mip-NeRF 360 | 9 scenes (4 indoor, 5 outdoor) | Object-centric, unbounded real-world scenes. The Hugging Face repo (`ethanweber/Mip-NeRF_360_Processed_with_Nerfstudio`) lacks official metadata/READMEs, offering only raw zip archives. | 3D | 360-degree orbit, multi-view | Real | `.zip` archives containing processed images. | Novel View Synthesis for static 3D scenes |
| Tanks & Temples | 16 core scenes | High-resolution video sequences and images of complex environments. As per the `matchnerf` README, this dataset is heavily utilized for Explicit Correspondence Matching for Generalizable NeRFs (TPAMI 2025). | 3D | Free-moving multi-view (video/images) | Real | Organized into `images/` and `sparse/` (COLMAP), with a central `database.db`. | 3D Surface Reconstruction / SLAM |
| Deep Blending | 13 scenes | Real-world indoor and outdoor environments sourced from the `JunfengNi/G4Splat` repo. Each scene contains multi-view images (12-418 images), camera poses, refined depth maps, and global textured meshes. | 3D | Multi-view | Real | Multi-view `images/`, `sparse/` COLMAP poses, and `split-5views.json`. | Novel View Synthesis / Image-based Rendering |
| D-NeRF | 7 synthetic scenes | 200-frame animations of moving objects with rigid, articulated, and non-rigid deformations generated from a monocular moving camera. | 4D | Monocular moving camera | Synthetic | 2D image sequences (e.g. `r_000.png`) mapped to timestamps via JSON. | Dynamic Novel View Synthesis |
| NeRF-DS | 7 scenes | Monocular video sequences of dynamic specular (reflective) objects with foreground masks and camera parameters. | 4D | Monocular video | Real | Monocular video frames with timestamps, camera poses, and masks. | Dynamic Novel View Synthesis for specular/reflective objects |
| Neu3D | 5 scenes | Real-world multi-view dynamic scenes featuring interacting foregrounds and complex non-rigid motion, captured via a multi-view camera rig. | 4D | Multi-view synchronized camera rig | Real | Multi-view video streams with calibrated camera parameters per frame/timestep. | 4D Novel View Synthesis / Dynamic 3D Reconstruction |
| LLFF | 8 scenes | Forward-facing, real-world scenes. We extracted highly detailed `*-nbv.json` metadata files from the `nerfbaselines-data` repo. | 3D | Forward-facing, sparse-view | Real | JSON files map strict 3x4 `pose` matrices, focal/center `intrinsics`, and `image_size` (1008x756). | Novel View Synthesis for forward-facing scenes |
| BungeeNeRF | 12 scenes | Multi-scale imagery (satellite to ground-level) of cityscapes acquired using Google Earth Studio. | 3D | Multi-scale (satellite to ground-level) | Synthetic | N/A | Multi-scale Novel View Synthesis |
| Replica | 18 scenes | Highly photo-realistic 3D indoor scene reconstructions with dense meshes, high-resolution HDR textures, and semantic/instance annotations (sourced from `G4Splat`). | 3D | Scanned meshes (rendered from any view) | Scanned | Contains `images/`, `gt_mesh/`, `traj.txt`, and `split-5views.json`. | 3D Semantic Segmentation / Embodied AI Navigation |
| MonoDyGauBench | 50 scenes | Standardized dynamic 3DGS benchmark dataset consisting of monocular videos (incorporates subsets of datasets like D-NeRF and HyperNeRF). | 4D | Monocular moving camera | Both (Mostly Real) | Video sequences with normalized timestamps and poses mapped via JSON. | Benchmarking Dynamic 3D Gaussian Splatting |
| HyperNeRF | Various scenes | Features complex non-rigid motions and topological changes. The `Mask-Benchmark` README (from *TRASE*, 2024) provides GT segmentation masks, while the official Google release provides the raw RGB. | 4D | Monocular moving camera | Real | Uses `dataset.json` (train/test splits) and OpenCV pinhole distortion. | Dynamic Novel View Synthesis for topologically varying scenes |
| Nerfies | 9 scenes | Monocular videos captured using a handheld camera, featuring non-rigidly deforming scenes (primarily human subjects). | 4D | Monocular moving camera (handheld) | Real | Monocular video sequences where each frame corresponds to a specific timestamp/pose. | Dynamic Novel View Synthesis for non-rigidly deforming scenes |
| iPhone | Varies (e.g., 14-16) | Real-world casual mobile captures using iPhones. Features typical challenges like motion blur, rolling shutter, and sparse views. | Varies | Monocular moving camera (handheld iPhone) | Real | Video frames with timestamps (if dynamic). | Benchmarking mobile captures / Novel View Synthesis |
| CMU Panoptic | 65 sequences | Massively multiview captures (using hundreds of cameras) of 3D human motion and social interactions, including 3D skeletons and calibration data. | 4D | Massively multiview (480 VGA, 31 HD cameras) | Real | Hardware-synchronized multi-view videos and 3D skeletons per frame/timestep. | 3D Human Pose Estimation / Social Interaction Modeling |
| Hi4D | 100 sequences | Multi-view images, 4D textured scans, segmentation masks, and parametric body models of physically close human-human interactions. | 4D | Multi-view (8 RGB cameras) | Real | High-fidelity 4D textured raw scans (meshes) for each frame, along with registered SMPL models and segmentation masks. | Dynamic Surface Reconstruction / 4D Human Interaction Analysis |
